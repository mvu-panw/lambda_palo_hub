Strata Cloud Manager

A cloud-based console for managing and operating your whole network security estate. It manages PA-Series and VM-Series firewalls, Cloud NGFW, Prisma SASE, and Prisma AIRS AI Runtime Security, and collects telemetry from every enforcement point.

  • Consistent policy everywhere: hierarchical folders and reusable snippets let one change apply to firewalls and SASE alike. The datasheet cites up to 50% lower operating costs.
  • Real-time visibility: Command Center shows users, apps, devices, and top threats, then guides you from a threat to its source and to how to block it.
  • Stronger security posture: AI analysis finds shadowed, redundant, overly permissive, and unused rules, and checks configuration against best practices and compliance standards.
  • Predictive operations: AIOps forecasts capacity, health, CVE, and connectivity issues (130+ categories) up to 90 days ahead.
  • Built in: ADEM for monitoring the user-to-application experience, and Strata Copilot, a natural-language assistant for finding and fixing issues fast.

Next-Generation Trust Security

Certificate lifecycle management and private PKI that run inside Strata Cloud Manager, next to the firewall, VPN, and decryption policies that depend on those certificates.

  • Discovery: continuously inventories certificates across servers, workloads, Kubernetes clusters, endpoints, and network appliances, tracking expiry, ownership, and issuer.
  • Lifecycle automation: issuance, renewal, deployment, rotation, and key installation, aligned to change processes.
  • Works with any CA: integrates with multiple public and private CAs, and reissues in bulk if a CA is distrusted.
  • SaaS private PKI: HSM-backed keys, flexible root and intermediate hierarchies, ACME, EST, and SCEP enrollment, and Microsoft auto-enrollment, with high availability across regions.
  • Why it matters: public TLS certificate lifetimes keep getting shorter. An expired certificate can take down NGFW management, GlobalProtect, SSL decryption, or SASE services.
