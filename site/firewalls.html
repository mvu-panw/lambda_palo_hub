PA-5500 Series

Download datasheet ↓

A quantum-optimized NGFW for high-speed data centers, internet gateways, and service providers. It is built on the FE-400 ASIC for high throughput and low latency, and supports NGFW clustering for high availability at scale.

  • Models: PA-5540, PA-5550, PA-5560, PA-5570, PA-5580
  • Data ports, PA-5540/5550: 10G/25G SFP28 (16), 40G/100G QSFP28 (16), 100G/400G QSFP-DD (4)
  • Data ports, PA-5560/5570/5580: 10G/25G SFP28 (8), 40G/100G QSFP28 (12), 100G/400G QSFP-DD (8)
  • HSCI: 100G/400G QSFP-DD (2), plus dedicated 10G log ports
  • Chassis: 3U, 19" rack; 2,100 W average / 3,100 W max; front-to-back airflow
  • Post-quantum: PQC decryption and VPN, plus a PCIe slot reserved for future post-quantum capabilities

PA-5400 Series

Download datasheet ↓

A data center and internet-edge NGFW with built-in web proxy, so firewall and proxy run on one platform with one policy.

  • Models: PA-5410, PA-5420, PA-5430, PA-5440, PA-5445
  • Data ports: 1G/2.5G/5G/10G (8), 1G/10G SFP/SFP+ (12), 1G/10G/25G SFP28 (4), 40G/100G QSFP28 (4)
  • High availability: 1G/10G SFP+ (2) plus 40G QSFP+ (1)
  • Web proxy: explicit (PAC) and transparent, up to 250K sessions and 100K unique IPs
  • Chassis: 2U, 19" rack; 630 W average / 760 W max; 1:1 redundant 1,200 W power supplies

PA-3400 Series

Download datasheet ↓

A 1U NGFW with native web proxy, 100G options on the larger models, and active/active or active/passive high availability. It also gives visibility into unmanaged IoT devices without extra sensors.

  • Models: PA-3410, PA-3420, PA-3430, PA-3440
  • Data ports, all models: 1G/2.5G/5G/10G (12), 1G/10G SFP/SFP+ (10), 25G SFP28 (4)
  • Additional ports, PA-3430/3440: 40G/100G QSFP28 (2)
  • Hardware: 480 GB SSD; TPM for secure boot and hardware root of trust
  • Chassis: 1U, 19" rack; 133 W average / 190 W max; redundant 450 W AC power supplies

PA-1400 Series

Download datasheet ↓

A 1U NGFW with native web proxy and PoE. Optional ZeroTouch Provisioning (ZTP) simplifies rolling out large numbers of firewalls.

  • Models: PA-1410, PA-1420
  • PA-1410 ports: 10/100/1000 (8), 1G/2.5G/5G PoE (4), 1G SFP (6), 1G/10G SFP/SFP+ (4)
  • PA-1420 ports: 10/100/1000 (4), 1G/2.5G/5G (4), 1G/2.5G/5G PoE (4), 1G SFP (2), 1G/10G SFP/SFP+ (8)
  • PoE budget: 151 W total, up to 90 W on a single port
  • Chassis: 1U, 19" rack; 450 W AC power supply, with an optional second

Shared across every series

Prevention

  • Precision AI: AI-driven threat analysis and prevention in real time.
  • Advanced Threat Prevention: inline IPS that stops evasive and zero-day attacks.
  • Single-pass architecture: networking, policy lookup, App-ID, decoding, and signature matching run once per packet. Performance stays predictable with every service enabled. Download the brief.
  • Decryption: inspects inbound and outbound SSL/TLS, with policy-based control over what gets decrypted.

Operations

  • Post-quantum cryptography: PQC SSL/TLS decryption, PQC site-to-site VPN, and cipher translation, using NIST ML-KEM, ML-DSA, and SLH-DSA.
  • Identity-based policy: ties traffic to users across on-premises and cloud identity stores, with MFA enforcement for credential protection.
  • Management: Strata Cloud Manager or Panorama, with AI-powered posture and health insights.
  • Scale and resilience: HA pairs or NGFW clustering. See the clustering migration guide.
