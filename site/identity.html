Privileged Access Management (PAM)

Secures every privileged identity (admins, developers, contractors, vendors, and service accounts) across cloud, infrastructure, and web apps. It covers traditional PAM (credential vaulting and rotation) as well as zero-standing-privilege access. FedRAMP compliant.

  • Zero standing privileges (ZSP) and just-in-time (JIT) access: access is granted only when needed and removed when the session ends.
  • Native cloud access: developers reach AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud with their native tools and no static credentials.
  • Infrastructure access: agentless, VPN-less sessions to Windows, Linux, Kubernetes, and databases, using signed SSH certificates for Linux and temporary local users for Windows.
  • Vaulting and rotation: policy-based rotation of passwords and secrets keeps you audit-ready.
  • Discovery and attack-path mapping: continuous scanning for unmanaged accounts, plus AI recommendations for onboarding them at scale.
  • Identity threat response: detects in-progress identity attacks and automatically ends compromised sessions. Web app sessions are recorded and searchable.

Endpoint Privilege Manager (EPM)

Zero trust for the endpoint: removes permanent admin rights from users, applications, and AI agents on Linux, macOS, and Windows without flooding the helpdesk.

  • Remove local admin: revokes standing admin rights, the main fuel for lateral movement.
  • Policy-based elevation: raises the privileges of specific approved processes, not the user, so people can still get work done.
  • Application control and ringfencing: only vetted software runs, and approved apps can't reach sensitive files, network locations, or other processes' memory. This blocks living-off-the-land attacks.
  • Secure sign-in: ties endpoint login to your identity provider with phishing-resistant MFA.
  • AI agents included: agents and their toolchains are held to the same least-privilege rules as people.
  • Reported results: 74% fewer overprivileged accounts and 57% fewer successful account takeovers.

Identity Governance & Administration (IGA)

Continuous, automated governance of who has access to what, and why, covering both standard and privileged access on the same platform.

  • One system of record: every identity, application, and entitlement in one view. It flags toxic combinations, excessive permissions, and orphaned accounts.
  • Automated access reviews: end-to-end certification campaigns with auditor-ready evidence. AI pre-approvals cut repetitive reviews, and privileged accounts are reviewed in the same process.
  • Lifecycle automation: joiner, mover, and leaver changes run from AI-driven profiles, either through direct integrations or through your ITSM workflows.
  • Job-appropriate access: AI models roles from real usage, so new hires start with least-privileged access.
  • Modern access models: supports zero standing privilege and just-in-time access.
