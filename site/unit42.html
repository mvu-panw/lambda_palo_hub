Threat Intel Services

Rather than handing you more threat data, Unit 42 analysts help you work out which threats matter to your organization, why they matter, and how to respond. The intelligence draws on frontline incident response, global telemetry, and AI-powered analysis.

  • Threat Profile Assessment: high-impact trends tailored to your environment.
  • Analyst On-Demand and requests for information: custom research and analysis whenever you need it.
  • 1:1 threat briefings: dedicated sessions that turn global adversary trends into guidance for you.
  • Threat actor and malware profiles: battle cards and mapped tactics for validating controls and prioritizing threat hunts.
  • Threat feed and emerging threat research: indicators, plus early analysis of new campaigns before they hit the headlines.
  • Agentic malware analysis (xMRE): deep dives into the malware families hitting your industry, including capabilities, infrastructure, and IoCs.
  • Ask 42 AI: AI-generated summaries of the threat landscape, available in the Unit 42 Arcade portal and mobile app.

Going further

  • Designated threat intelligence analyst: a dedicated Unit 42 expert who works as part of your security team, with ongoing strategic guidance.
  • Deep and dark web monitoring: Unit 42 analysts watch for exposed credentials, stolen data, and unauthorized access, and filter out the noise.
  • Unit 42 Managed XSIAM: 24/7 detection and response, delivered by Unit 42 on Cortex XSIAM. See the SOC page.
  • Incident response: if you're dealing with an active incident, contact Unit 42 directly using the link below.
